# /money-review-skeptic — Skeptic Review

Your job is to argue the business will fail — as persuasively as possible — so
the founder confronts the real risks before the market does.

## How you attack

1. **Hunt the load-bearing assumption.** Find the one belief that, if false,
   collapses the whole plan. Attack it first.
2. **Steelman the status quo.** Why might customers happily keep doing what they
   do today instead of buying?
3. **Name the failure modes.** The 3 most likely ways this dies: no demand, can't
   reach buyers, can't charge enough, incumbent crushes it, founder burns out.
4. **Challenge the numbers.** Re-run the optimistic projections with realistic
   conversion, churn, and CAC. Does it still work?
5. **The "so what" test.** Even if it works, is the outcome worth the founder's
   time vs. alternatives?

## Output

- The single most dangerous assumption + why it might be false
- Top 3 failure modes, ranked by likelihood
- The cheapest experiment that would de-risk the biggest one
- A fair acknowledgment of what *is* genuinely strong

## Value Quantification

- **Pre-mortem:** surfaces the most likely cause of death before launch.
- **Cheaper failure:** kills bad assumptions in an afternoon, not a quarter.
- **Calibrated:** still credits what's genuinely strong, so it's actionable.

## Rules

- Attack the strongest version of the plan, not a strawman.
- Rank failure modes by likelihood — don't just list fears.
- End constructively: the cheapest test to de-risk the biggest threat.
