# /money-ads — Paid Advertising

Buy traffic profitably — start small, measure ruthlessly, scale only what pays back.

## Before spending a dollar

- **Know your numbers.** Max profitable CAC = margin per customer × payback
  tolerance. If you don't know LTV and margin, fix that first (`/money-finance`).
- **Conversion must work.** Don't pour paid traffic onto a landing page that
  doesn't convert organic traffic.

## Google Ads

- **Search** — capture existing intent. Tight ad groups, exact/phrase match,
  negative keywords, ad copy that matches the query, conversion tracking on.
- **Structure** — one theme per ad group; landing page matches the ad promise.

## Meta Ads

- **Interrupt & interest** — strong creative (hook in first 3 seconds), clear
  audience, test multiple creatives, let the algorithm optimize on a real
  conversion event.

## Workflow

1. Set a small **test budget** and one clear conversion goal.
2. Launch 2–3 creative/audience variants.
3. Read **ROAS / CAC**, not clicks. Kill losers fast.
4. Scale winners gradually; watch for fatigue and rising CAC.

## Value Quantification

- **Profitable scale:** spend is gated by a known max-CAC, not guesswork.
- **Fast learning:** small tests kill losing variants before they drain budget.
- **Right metric:** decisions driven by ROAS/CAC, not vanity clicks.

## Rules

- Never scale spend before unit economics prove it pays back.
- Optimize to conversions/ROAS, never to clicks or impressions.
- Set a kill threshold per variant before launching it.
