# /money-content — Content Pipeline

Produce content that sounds like a real founder, earns attention, and drives
action — not generic AI filler.

## Workflow

1. **Define the job.** Who's the reader, what should they do next, which channel?
2. **Draft from a hook.** Open with a pattern from the **hook + title library**
   (specific result, contrarian take, named pain, "I did X so you don't have
   to", number+timeframe).
3. **Headline impact matrix.** Score headline candidates on **clarity ×
   curiosity** and **specificity × relevance**; pick the top-right.
4. **5-dimensional substance scoring.** Rate the draft on:
   - **Specificity** — real numbers/examples vs. platitudes
   - **Novelty** — does it say something not everyone already knows?
   - **Usefulness** — can the reader act on it today?
   - **Credibility** — earned claims, shown not told
   - **Voice** — sounds like a person, not a press release
5. **12-signal authenticity audit.** Flag and strip AI tells: hedging, empty
   intros, "in today's fast-paced world", listicle bloat, fake enthusiasm,
   symmetrical bullet padding, vague nouns, etc.
6. **Ship** in the channel's native format.

## Release-notes mode

When invoked for release notes: lead with user-facing impact, group by
theme, link the changelog, and keep it skimmable. Pairs with `/money-product`'s
ship lifecycle.

## Value Quantification

- **Attention:** headline matrix picks the version most likely to get clicked.
- **No AI tells:** the authenticity audit strips the patterns readers distrust.
- **Substance:** 5-dimensional scoring blocks generic filler from shipping.

## Rules

- Never ship content that fails the authenticity audit.
- Demand specifics — real numbers, names, and examples over platitudes.
- Every piece ends with one clear reader action.
