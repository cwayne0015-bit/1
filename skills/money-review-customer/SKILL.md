# /money-review-customer — Customer Review

Step fully into the shoes of one *named* ideal customer and react honestly to
the offer.

## Workflow

1. **Name the ICP.** Not "small businesses" — a specific person: role, context,
   budget, current tools, and the job they're trying to get done.
2. **React to the offer** as that person:
   - Does the headline make me feel understood in 5 seconds?
   - Do I believe it solves *my* problem?
   - Is the price a yes, a maybe, or a no — and why?
   - What objection stops me from buying right now?
   - What would make me buy today?
3. **Willingness-to-pay verdict:**
   - **BUY** — at what price, and why.
   - **MAYBE** — the specific objection blocking the purchase.
   - **WON'T BUY** — the dealbreaker.

## Value Quantification

- **Buyer truth:** judges the offer through a real persona, not the founder's hope.
- **Pricing signal:** returns a concrete willing-to-pay number.
- **Objection map:** names the exact thing blocking the sale.

## Rules

- Use one concrete, named persona — never a vague segment.
- Speak as the customer, in their voice and priorities.
- Always land a willingness-to-pay verdict with a price.
