# /money-strategy — Strategy & Market Research

Produce a rigorous, decision-ready strategy document — and, with `iterate`,
sharpen an existing business after product–market fit.

## Workflow

1. **Term-by-term clarity audit.** Define every fuzzy word in the user's pitch
   ("creators", "affordable", "AI-powered"). Ambiguous terms hide bad
   assumptions.
2. **Fuzzy → measurable goals.** Convert "grow fast" into numbers: target MRR,
   customers, conversion rate, timeline.
3. **Premise deconstruction.** Surface the core assumptions the business rests
   on. Mark each as validated, plausible, or untested.
4. **Market research.** TAM/SAM/SOM sanity check, segments, trends, real
   competitors and their pricing.
5. **SWOT** — strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats.
6. **4P** — product, price, place, promotion.
7. **Business-model stress test.** Unit economics: CAC, LTV, margin, payback.
   Does the math survive realistic numbers?
8. **Go-to-market plan.** First channel, first 10 customers, week-one motion.

## `iterate` mode (post-PMF)

When invoked with `iterate`, skip greenfield framing. Instead: identify the
current binding constraint (acquisition, retention, monetization, or margin),
form one hypothesis to relax it, and define the metric that proves it.

## Value Quantification

- **Clarity:** every fuzzy term is defined before money is spent on it.
- **Survivable math:** unit economics are stress-tested, not assumed.
- **Actionable:** ends with a concrete first-channel GTM motion.

## Output

- Strategy report (clarity audit → premise → SWOT → 4P → model → GTM)
- Tomorrow's first action: [specific task]

## Rules

- Do not let fuzzy language pass — define or quantify it.
- Stress-test unit economics with pessimistic numbers, not best-case.
- Distinguish validated assumptions from untested ones explicitly.
