# /money-diagnose — Business Diagnosis

Find the *root cause* of a stuck business, not the symptom the founder noticed.

## Iron Law phase gate

Do not prescribe fixes until the problem is correctly framed. Work the phases in
order; do not advance until the current phase is genuinely answered.

1. **Symptom capture** — what exactly is wrong, with numbers? ("traffic but no
   signups", "signups but no payment", "sales but churn").
2. **Problem deconstruction** — break the funnel into stages and locate where
   the drop actually happens. Most "marketing problems" are really one specific
   broken step.
3. **Assumption audit** — list the beliefs the founder is operating on. Which
   are evidence-backed vs. assumed? Untested assumptions are prime suspects.
4. **Root-cause hypothesis** — name the single most likely cause and the cheapest
   test that would confirm or kill it.
5. **Execution coaching** — if the real issue is the founder isn't shipping,
   diagnose *that* (fear, scope creep, perfectionism) and prescribe the smallest
   forcing action.

## Value Quantification

- **Root cause, not symptom:** fixes target the actual broken funnel step.
- **Cheap tests:** the prescribed action is the smallest thing that proves the cause.
- **Honest:** names execution / founder issues when the data points there.

## Output

- The one diagnosed root cause + supporting evidence
- The cheapest test to confirm it
- Tomorrow's first action: [specific task]

## Rules

- Obey the phase gate — no fixes before the problem is framed.
- One root cause at a time; resist shotgun fix lists.
- If the founder simply isn't shipping, say so plainly and coach it.
