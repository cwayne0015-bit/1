# /money-discover — Idea Discovery & Validation

Turn a vague "I want to make money" into one validated, narrow, shippable bet.

## Workflow

1. **Anchor to the founder.** Pull the profile (skills, reach, constraints,
   unfair advantages). Ideas must fit what *this* founder can ship.
2. **Scan for gaps.** Look for under-served niches, painful workflows, pricing
   gaps, and audiences the founder already touches.
3. **Run the 6 forcing questions** (demand validation):
   1. Who exactly has this problem, and how often?
   2. What do they do today instead (the status quo)?
   3. Would they pay — and how much — to make it go away?
   4. Can you reach them cheaply and repeatedly?
   5. Why hasn't someone already solved this well?
   6. What's the smallest version that delivers value this week?
4. **Score with the 5 filters** (1–5 each):
   - **Demand** — is the pain real and frequent?
   - **Reachability** — can you get in front of buyers cheaply?
   - **Willingness to pay** — money on the table, not just interest?
   - **Founder fit** — can *you* build and sell this?
   - **Moat / wedge** — why you, why now, why not the incumbent?
5. **Competitive-intelligence protocol.** Name real competitors, what they
   charge, where they're weak, and the wedge you exploit. No hand-waving.
6. **Output the narrowest bet.** One sentence: *the smallest thing you can ship
   tomorrow that someone would pay for.*

## Value Quantification

- **De-risked:** ideas are killed cheaply *before* you build them.
- **Focused:** ends with one narrow bet, not a list of maybes.
- **Honest:** competitive intel forces a real wedge, not wishful thinking.

## Output

- Top 1–3 scored opportunities with the filter breakdown
- The chosen narrowest-bet statement
- Tomorrow's first action: [specific task]

## Rules

- Reject ideas the founder can't actually reach or build.
- Name real competitors with real prices — no vague "the market is big".
- Always end on a single tomorrow-shippable narrowest bet.
