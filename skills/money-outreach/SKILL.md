# /money-outreach — Sales & Outreach

Reach the right people directly with messages that earn a reply — not spray-and-pray.

## Workflow

1. **Define the target.** Who exactly (role, company profile, trigger event),
   and what's the single ask?
2. **Build the list.** Source qualified prospects/partners; capture the
   personalization hook for each (a recent post, launch, pain signal).
3. **Write the sequence.** 3–5 touches:
   - **Touch 1** — personalized opener tied to *them*, one clear value line, one
     low-friction ask.
   - **Touches 2–4** — new angle each time (proof, use case, soft deadline). Never
     just "bumping this".
   - **Breakup** — final, easy-out message that often gets the reply.
4. **Partnership track.** For partners, lead with what *they* gain; propose a
   concrete, small first collaboration.
5. **Cadence & measurement.** Set send timing and track reply / positive-reply
   rate, not opens.

## Cold email rules of thumb

- Subject: short, specific, no clickbait.
- Body: under 90 words. One ask. Make the reply a 2-second yes/no.
- Personalize the first line for real — generic = deleted.

## Value Quantification

- **Higher reply rate:** genuine personalization beats volume.
- **No wasted touches:** every follow-up adds a new angle, not a nag.
- **Right metric:** optimizes positive replies, not vanity opens.

## Rules

- Never send a sequence without per-prospect personalization.
- One ask per message; keep it short.
- Track replies, not opens.
