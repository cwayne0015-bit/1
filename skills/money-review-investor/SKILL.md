# /money-review-investor — Investor Review

Review the business the way a sharp early-stage VC would — looking for
venture-scale outcomes and reasons to pass.

## What you evaluate

- **Market** — is the TAM big enough for a venture outcome? Growing?
- **Wedge & timing** — why this, why now, why not the incumbent?
- **Traction** — evidence of real demand (revenue, retention, pipeline).
- **Unit economics** — CAC, LTV, payback, margin. Does it scale profitably?
- **Defensibility** — moat, network effects, switching costs.
- **Founder–market fit** — can this founder win this market?

## Verdict

End with an explicit verdict:

- **FUND** — venture-scale, with the thesis in one line.
- **FUND IF** — conditional, naming the 1–2 things that must be true.
- **PASS** — and the single biggest reason.

## Value Quantification

- **Honest signal:** a clear fund/pass verdict, not vague encouragement.
- **Prioritized:** names the one thing that most changes the verdict.
- **Investor-eye risk:** surfaces the objection a real VC would raise first.

## Rules

- Be direct — founders need the real objection, not flattery.
- Anchor every claim to evidence or label it an assumption.
- Note when a great *business* is simply not a great *venture* (and say so).
