# Off-Market Sourcing Campaign Plan — South Florida Wholesale Spreads
### NW Pompano/Sanders Park (33060) · Lauderhill (33313) · Miami Gardens/Carol City (33056) · Riviera Beach Heights (33404)

**Premise:** Retail MLS in these markets prices near ARV, so spreads come from *off-market motivated sellers*. This plan pulls leads directly from county public records (lawful under FL Ch. 119), stacks/scrubs them, and runs compliant outreach.

**Compliance baked in throughout:** Outreach is honest and respectful. You disclose you are a **real estate investor/wholesaler, not a licensed agent**, and that you **intend to buy or assign the contract**. All texts/calls follow **DNC / TCPA and Florida's FTSA** (you need prior express written consent for autodialed/mass texts; manual one-to-one outreach is safer for cold contacts; honor STOP within 15 days). Email follows **CAN-SPAM** (real ID, physical address, working opt-out). You make **no price or closing promises** at outreach stage — that is underwriting/contract. All numbers below are **conservative planning estimates, not warranties. Not legal advice — confirm FTSA/TCPA approach with counsel before any SMS/dialer use.**

## 1. Per-Submarket Lead Sources + Exact County Data Source

### A. NW Pompano Beach / Sanders Park — 33060 (Broward)
**Submarket angle:** Older 1950s–60s CBS (concrete block) single-story stock, many long-tenure owners with massive equity; pockets of tired landlords and inherited homes. Deferred-maintenance roofs (insurance pressure) is a strong motivator here.

| Lead type | Where to pull it |
|---|---|
| Absentee / out-of-state owners | **BCPA** (bcpa.net / web.bcpa.net) — owner mailing address ≠ situs address; pull by ZIP/neighborhood |
| High-equity long-tenure | BCPA sales history (last sale 15+ yrs ago, low/no recent mortgage) cross-checked w/ **Official Records AcclaimWeb** (officialrecords.broward.org/AcclaimWeb) for open mortgages/satisfactions |
| Pre-foreclosure / lis pendens | **AcclaimWeb**, doc type "LIS PENDENS" / "NOTICE OF FORECLOSURE" |
| Tax-delinquent | **Broward Tax Collector** (browardtax.org; county-taxes.net/broward) "Full Bill History" → flags tax-deed scheduling |
| Code violations / liens | **City of Pompano Beach** lien search (pompanobeachfl.gov building-inspections/lien-search); Broward CES 954-357-9794 |
| Probate / inherited | **AcclaimWeb** (deeds: "PERSONAL REP DEED", recent death-related transfers) + Broward Clerk probate |
| Owner LLCs (skip to human) | **Sunbiz** (search.sunbiz.org → by officer/registered agent) |

## 2. Prioritized 30-Day Action Plan (Solo Operator, Modest Budget)

**List priority (highest motivation × highest equity first):**
1. **Pre-foreclosure / lis pendens** (urgent + equity) — pull from each county clerk/AcclaimWeb.
2. **Tax-delinquent** (2+ yrs = near tax-deed pressure).
3. **Code violations + absentee** (stacked = tired landlord, top tier).
4. **Probate / inherited deeds** (last 6–18 months).
5. **High-equity long-tenure absentee** (the big bulk list; lower urgency, great economics).

**Stack & scrub workflow (do in this order):**
1. **Pull** each list by ZIP from the county sources above; export to one spreadsheet per county.
2. **Dedupe** across lists by parcel/folio ID (a lead on 3 lists = priority).
3. **Equity filter:** keep where (assessed value − recorded mortgage balances) ≥ ~50% equity; drop clearly over-leveraged.
4. **Absentee filter:** flag mailing address ≠ situs and out-of-state.
5. **Owner resolution:** LLC-owned → Sunbiz to find the human officer/agent.
6. **Skip-trace** for phones/emails (batch service).
7. **Compliance scrub:** run phone list against **National DNC + Florida DNC**, suppress litigators/known opt-outs, and treat all numbers as requiring **manual, one-to-one** contact unless you have prior express written consent (FTSA). Maintain a master suppression/opt-out log.

**Weekly cadence (solo, lean):**
- **Week 1 — Build:** Pull lis pendens + tax-delinquent + code/absentee (all 4 counties). Dedupe, equity/absentee filter. Skip-trace top ~300. Send **Mail Batch 1** (highest-tier ~150–200 pieces).
- **Week 2 — Pull #2 + work responses:** Pull probate + high-equity long-tenure absentee. Begin **manual cold-call/text** on skip-traced top tier (small daily blocks, ~30–50 dials/day). Screen inbound from Mail Batch 1.
- **Week 3 — Follow-up:** **Mail Batch 2** to non-responders from Batch 1 + new probate/long-tenure list. Continue manual calls. Advance hot leads to lead-scoring → underwriting.
- **Week 4 — Optimize:** Third touch on best segment, double-down on whichever list/submarket pulled best response, retire dead leads, refresh suppression list. Plan next month's pulls.
- **Steady state:** Aim ~3 touches per lead across channels over 30–45 days; mail + manual call/text is the cheapest high-yield combo for a solo operator.

## 3. Outreach Templates (Compliant — disclose investor intent, opt-out)

### Direct-Mail Piece (postcard/letter)
> Hi [OWNER NAME],
>
> My name is **[YOUR NAME]** and I'm a **local real estate investor** (not a real estate agent). I buy houses in **[NEIGHBORHOOD]** directly from owners, often as-is — no repairs, no cleanouts, no agent commissions.
>
> If you've ever thought about selling **[PROPERTY ADDRESS]**, I'd welcome a no-pressure conversation about whether a direct sale might work for you. I can't promise a specific price until I learn about the home, but I'll be straight with you either way.
>
> Call or text me at **[PHONE]** or email **[EMAIL]**. If you'd rather not hear from me again, just let me know and I'll remove you from my list.
>
> — [YOUR NAME], [COMPANY], [PHYSICAL MAILING ADDRESS]
>
> *I'm an investor who may purchase the property or assign the purchase contract to another buyer.*

*(CAN-SPAM/transparency: real name, company, physical address, and opt-out included.)*

### SMS Opener (manual, one-to-one — FTSA-aware)
> Hi [NAME], this is [YOUR NAME], a local home buyer/investor (not an agent). I'm interested in possibly buying [STREET]. No pressure — would you ever consider a cash, as-is offer? Reply STOP to opt out anytime.

*(Send manually one-to-one. Honor STOP within 15 days. Do not use an autodialer/mass-texting platform without documented prior express written consent. Identify yourself and disclose investor intent.)*

### Cold-Call Opener
> "Hi, is this [NAME]? My name is [YOUR NAME] — I'm a **local real estate investor, not an agent**, and I'm calling because I buy houses directly in **[NEIGHBORHOOD]**. I want to be upfront: I may buy your home myself or assign the contract to another buyer. I'm not calling to list it. Is now an okay time for a quick question — have you ever thought about selling **[ADDRESS]**? … No worries either way, and if you'd like, I'll add you to my do-not-call list right now."

*(Check DNC before dialing; honor opt-out immediately and log it; no price/close promises.)*

## 4. Lead-Scoring Quick-Filter (Fast Call Screen → Underwriting)

Score the 5 M's on the call; **advance to underwriting if 3+ are strong, including Motivation.**

| Factor | Quick question | Advance signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Motivation** | "What's got you thinking about selling?" | Real reason: foreclosure, tired landlord, inherited, relocating, repairs, taxes |
| **Condition** | "How's the roof/AC/kitchen — anything needing work?" | Deferred maintenance / as-is (= spread) |
| **Timeline** | "If the number worked, how soon would you want to close?" | 30–60 days or "ASAP" |
| **Price flexibility** | "Do you have a number in mind, or open to a fair as-is offer?" | Open / realistic, not retail |
| **Equity / debt** | "Roughly what's owed on it?" | Low/no mortgage = room to make a deal |

**Auto-advance:** distressed (foreclosure/tax/probate) + high equity + flexible + as-is.
**Park/nurture:** motivated but over-leveraged, or unrealistic price (follow up later).
**Drop:** retail price expectation, no motivation, low equity + high payoff.
*(This is a screen, not an offer — never quote price or promise to close on the call.)*
