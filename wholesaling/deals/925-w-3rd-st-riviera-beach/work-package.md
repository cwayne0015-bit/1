## 3. Objection-Handling (tailored)

*Hold the open at $195K. If pressed, move in small steps only — and NEVER above ~$205K, never reveal the ceiling. If they push past ~$205K, say you'll "take it back to your buyer" and escalate to the human.*

**"The price already dropped — the seller won't go lower."**
> "Totally fair, and I respect that they've come down to meet the market. The difference is what I'm bringing: a list price is a hope, my number is **cash in hand, as-is, no contingencies that blow up at the appraisal**. A retail buyer at $290K still needs financing on a 1960 home with a single bath that needs work — that's a hard appraisal and a long road. I'm certainty. Let's see if we can make $195 work, and if the seller's close, tell me what number ends it."

**"That's below the cuts they've already made."**
> "Understood — and that's the as-is cash discount, not a lowball for its own sake. After the interior rehab and carrying costs, $195 is where the math works for a buyer taking on the risk and timeline. If there's a realistic number that gets it done, I have a little room — what would it take?" *(Only then consider a small bump, e.g. to ~$198–200K, never toward the ceiling.)*

**"It's basically move-in — new roof and A/C."**
> "And I gave them full credit for that — it's exactly why I'm interested and why my number isn't lower. But roof and A/C are the *systems*; it's still a 1960 three-one with one bath that the listing itself says needs TLC, marketed to investors. Kitchen, bath, finishes, possible electrical/plumbing updates — that's where my budget goes. I'm paying for what's done and pricing the rest honestly."

**"Are you an agent? A wholesaler?"**
> "Good question — I'm a **principal buyer, not an agent**. And yes, I buy with the **right to assign**, meaning I may close it myself or bring a partner buyer — either way you get a signed contract, earnest money, and a clean close. Full transparency is how I work."

**"We have other offers."**
> "That's great — then you've got options, which is good for your seller. I'd just ask you weigh mine on **certainty**: cash, as-is, no financing or appraisal contingency, flexible close. If another offer is higher but financed, it carries appraisal and lender risk on an older home. Keep me as your clean backup at minimum — and if there's a number that makes me the easy yes, tell me."

## 5. Pre-Offer Diligence Checklist (this property)

Run alongside/just after the offer; firm up before going hard / removing contingencies.

- [ ] **FEMA flood zone** — pull the parcel on **FEMA MSC (Map Service Center / National Flood Hazard Layer)** and cross-check Palm Beach County flood maps. East 33404 is flood-prone; confirm zone (A/AE/VE vs. X), BFE, and **get an insurance quote** — flood premium can swing end-buyer math and your assignment appeal.
- [ ] **Roof** — verify the **2023 roof** via a closed/finalized **roof permit** with City of Riviera Beach Building Dept; confirm permit dates and final inspection. Get roof age/condition into the **4-point inspection** (insurers require it on a 1960 home).
- [ ] **Wind mitigation** — order a **wind-mit inspection** (openings, roof deck attachment, roof-to-wall); drives insurability and premium for the end-buyer.
- [ ] **A/C (2024)** — confirm install via permit and/or invoice; note SEER/condition.
- [ ] **Open permits / code** — search City of Riviera Beach for **open or expired permits, code-enforcement liens/violations, unpermitted work**. Open permits can stall closing.
- [ ] **Title & liens** — order a title search / commitment via [TITLE COMPANY]: ownership, mortgages, municipal/utility liens, code liens, judgments, taxes owed, any HOA. Confirm seller has authority to convey (estate? probate?).
- [ ] **Contractor walkthrough** — get a real GC on-site to confirm the **~$55,000 rehab** scope (kitchen, single bath, electrical, plumbing, flooring, interior). Treat $55K as conservative-but-unverified until confirmed.
- [ ] **County record** — PAPA parcel confirm: legal description, lot ~4,792 sqft, year built 1960, living area ~1,160 sqft, current taxes/assessment.
- [ ] **Comps refresh** — re-validate the **$375K ARV** with current sold comps in Riviera Beach Heights (similar 3/1–3/2 CBS, post-rehab). ARV is the spine of the deal and end-buyer margin.
