## 4. MAO (Maximum Allowable Offer)

**Formula:** MAO = (ARV × Buyer's Discount) − Repairs − Assignment Fee

- ARV = **$415,000** (conservative)
- Buyer's Discount = **70%** (standard fix-and-flip rule; **no upward adjustment** — Pompano is a *buyer's market* with ~82.5% selling below list and softening price/sqft, so 70% is appropriate and I would not stretch to 75%)
- Repairs = **$155,000**
- Assignment Fee = **$15,000** (midpoint of the $10K–$20K range)

```
MAO = (415,000 × 0.70) − 155,000 − 15,000
MAO = 290,500 − 155,000 − 15,000
MAO = $120,500
```

> ### MAO (to seller): **≈ $120,000**

## 5. Spread Check — Does Everyone Eat?

At the **MAO of $120,000**, with a $15K assignment fee, the wholesaler assigns the contract to an end buyer at **$135,000 all-in acquisition**.

| Party | Math | Result |
|---|---|---|
| **Wholesaler** | Assignment fee | **+$15,000** |

Both parties have room **only at MAO**. The flipper's ~$75K cushion is the minimum acceptable for a project this size; it absorbs the sensitivity stress below but not much more.
