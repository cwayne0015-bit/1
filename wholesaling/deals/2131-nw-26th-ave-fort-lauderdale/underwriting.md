## 4. MAO (Maximum Allowable Offer)

**Formula:** MAO = (ARV × Buyer's Discount) − Repairs − Assignment Fee

- **Buyer's discount: 70%** (baseline kept — market is *softening -7% YoY* and this is a full gut, which argues for staying strict).
- **ARV:** $330,000 (conservative)
- **Repairs:** $115,000 (high end)
- **Assignment fee:** **$15,000** (mid of $10–20K range; small-dollar ARV caps how much fee the spread can carry)

```
MAO = (330,000 × 0.70) − 115,000 − 15,000
    = 231,000 − 115,000 − 15,000
    = $101,000
```

**MAO ≈ $101,000.**

## 5. Spread Check (both parties must have room)

**End-buyer (flipper) economics at the MAO purchase:**
- Purchase (what end-buyer pays = MAO + assignment) = $101,000 + $15,000 = **$116,000**
- Repairs: $115,000
- Holding/selling costs ~12% of ARV: 0.12 × $330,000 = $39,600
- Total cost to flipper: 116,000 + 115,000 + 39,600 = **$270,600**
- Resale at ARV $330,000 → **end-buyer profit ≈ $59,400 (~18% of ARV).** ✅ Healthy room for the flipper.

**Wholesaler economics:**
- Contract at MAO ($101,000), assign for +$15,000 = **$15,000 fee.** ✅ Clean.

## 7. Recommendation

### Verdict: **NO-GO at list ($240K) — but a GO to PURSUE with a low offer.**

**The gap that defines the deal:**
- **List price: $240,000**
- **MAO (base): ~$101,000**
- **Walk-away ceiling: contract no higher than ~$115K** (above this the assignment fee evaporates and the flipper's margin thins below tolerance)

The asking price is **~$140K above MAO.** Despite this being a genuinely distressed, well-discounted listing (the best spread candidate found, far better than the Pompano NO-GO), **at $240K the numbers do not work for a wholesale assignment.** The discount that makes it interesting is still not deep enough relative to a full $115K gut on a small house in a softening market.

**Why pursue anyway (worth an offer, unlike the last one):**
1. Priced like real distress; seller may be motivated (small dollar amount, likely as-is/estate/REO).
2. A near-identical distressed 3/1 on this exact street pocket (2798 NW 20th St) **closed at $230,500** — confirming the pocket and the distressed-trade level.
3. Real end-buyer margin (~18%) exists *if* basis is right — so a buyer exists if the price is gotten right.

**Action — route to Acquisitions (not yet to Buyer-Relations):**
- **Open near MAO: ~$100,000 cash, as-is, quick close.** Realistic walk-away ceiling: **~$115,000.** Above ~$115K → **walk.**
- Make any contract contingent on: (a) physical inspection confirming rehab scope, (b) **title/permit search** (open permits/violations on a 1955 home), and (c) **flood-zone confirmation** (AH suspected). FL attorney to review.
- **Do not route to buyer-relations** until the property is under contract at ≤ ~$115K. At $240K it is not assignable.

**Honest bottom line:** Best real spread candidate in the target market right now, and it clears the screen *as a distressed property* — but it clears as a **deep-discount offer play, not as a list-price deal.** Make the low offer; if the seller won't move toward ~$115K or below, walk. At ask, there is no deal.
