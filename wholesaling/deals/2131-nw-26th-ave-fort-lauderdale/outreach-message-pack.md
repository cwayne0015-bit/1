### B2. Objection-Handling Lines

**"That's way too low."**
"I hear you — and I'm not here to insult anyone. On a **full-gut in flood zone AH** the rehab and carry eat the spread fast, so cash-as-is buyers like me land here. My trade-off is **certainty and speed, not price** — no contingencies, no repair credits, fast clean close. If the seller values a sure thing over chasing list, I'm a strong fit."

**"Seller wants list price."**
"Totally fair, and they may get a retail buyer who can wait out financing and inspections. My lane is the seller who'd rather **not gamble on that** — cash, as-is, no surprises. I'm happy to be the **backup** if a retail deal falls through on inspection or appraisal; that happens a lot on full-gut properties."

**"Are you an agent?"**
"No — I'm a **real-estate investor and the principal/buyer** here, not licensed. That's actually simpler for you: you're dealing **directly with the decision-maker**."

**"Is this assignable / are you a wholesaler?"**
"Yes — I buy with the **right to assign** to one of my cash buyers, and I'm upfront about it. Either I close myself or my assignee does, **same cash terms, same certainty** for your seller. I keep that transparent on purpose."

**"We have other offers."**
"Makes sense — strong area. Where mine stands out is **clean terms**: cash, as-is, no financing/appraisal/inspection contingencies, and a close date that works for the seller. If the others have financing or repair asks, I'd ask you to weigh **certainty against headline price**. Keep me in the running and I'll make it easy."

**"Why so low?"**
"It's not arbitrary — back into it from a **conservative resale**, subtract a **full-gut rehab**, flood-zone AH insurance, closing and carry costs, and a modest margin, and this is where a cash-as-is number lands. I'd rather give you a **real, defensible number I can close on** than a high one that dies in diligence."

*(Internal only — do not say: hold firm; you have room to step up if pressed, but never disclose the ceiling. Move in small increments and only if you get motivation/timeline signals.)*
