### Sender's checklist (do before hitting send)
- [ ] Contract signed, $100,000, "and/or assigns," 15-day inspection, title/permit/flood contingencies attached
- [ ] Proof of funds attached (recent bank statement or HML/transactional POF letter)
- [ ] Title/escrow company named
- [ ] Earnest money amount + deadline filled in
- [ ] MLS # and correct listing-agent contact confirmed (listing still active)
- [ ] FL attorney has reviewed your contract/assignment addendum at least once
- [ ] Tone: short, confident, flexible on the seller's timeline; certainty over price

### After you send
- Expect a rejection or a high counter — $100K vs. $240K is a wide gap; that's normal.
- **Walk-away ceiling: ~$115,000.** Above that the spread dies — do not chase.
- If they won't move toward ~$115K, politely pass and move to the next deal. No deal is a valid, correct outcome.
