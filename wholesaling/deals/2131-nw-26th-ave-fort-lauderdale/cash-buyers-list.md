## 4) Pull Recent Cash Buyers from Broward Public Records (exact workflow)

Goal: find LLCs that repeatedly bought houses **for cash** in 33311 + 33309/33312/33313/33304 recently, then get their contact via Sunbiz. This is the highest-signal, free way to find proven local buyers.

**Step 1 — Identify recent arm's-length cash sales (BCPA).**
- Broward County Property Appraiser: **bcpa.net** (search by address/owner/folio). Pull recent **sales history** in the target zips; note buyers (grantees) that are LLCs and sales with no mortgage recorded (a cash signal). Start from the subject parcel and "walk" nearby Rock Island sales.
- Sources: [BCPA.net](https://bcpa.net/) · [BCPA property search](https://web.bcpa.net/BcpaClient/)

**Step 2 — Confirm cash + find repeat grantees (Official Records).**
- Broward Records, Taxes & Treasury Official Records (AcclaimWeb): **https://officialrecords.broward.org/AcclaimWeb**
- Use these searches:
  - **Document Type = Deed (Warranty/Special Warranty Deed)** within a recent date range.
  - **Party Name (Grantee)** search to see how many properties an LLC bought (repeat buyers = active flippers/landlords).
  - **Consideration range** search to filter by price band (e.g., your sub-$200K acquisition range).
  - Cross-check: a deed with **no corresponding mortgage/lien recorded by the same grantee** ≈ cash purchase. (If a hard-money mortgage IS recorded, note the lender — that ties back to Section 2.)
- Records are fully searchable from 7/7/1977 forward; deeds are free public record.
- Sources: [Official Records AcclaimWeb](https://officialrecords.broward.org/AcclaimWeb) · [Broward public records search](https://www.broward.org/RecordsTaxesTreasury/Records/pages/publicrecordssearch.aspx) · [Official Records FAQ](https://www.broward.org/RecordsTaxesTreasury/FAQs/Pages/OfficialRecordsPublicSearch.aspx)

**Step 3 — Get the human behind the LLC (Sunbiz).**
- Florida Division of Corporations: search the LLC by name → find managers/officers, registered agent, principal/mailing address.
  - By entity name: **https://search.sunbiz.org/inquiry/corporationsearch/byname**
  - By officer/registered agent name (to find ALL entities a buyer controls): **https://search.sunbiz.org/Inquiry/CorporationSearch/ByOfficerOrRegisteredAgent** (format: Last, First).
- Sources: [Sunbiz by name](https://search.sunbiz.org/inquiry/corporationsearch/byname) · [Sunbiz by officer/agent](https://search.sunbiz.org/Inquiry/CorporationSearch/ByOfficerOrRegisteredAgent) · [Sunbiz search guides](https://dos.fl.gov/sunbiz/search/guides/corporation-records/)

**Step 4 — Build a "repeat buyer" shortlist.** Prioritize LLCs with 2+ recent cash purchases in the target zips. These are your warmest direct-mail / call targets because they've literally bought your exact product nearby.

## 5) Prioritized Outreach Plan (build the list fastest)

**Sequence (fastest signal first):**
1. **Public-records repeat cash buyers (Section 4)** — highest priority. They've bought your exact micro-product. Mail/call to introduce yourself and invite onto deal alerts.
2. **Local independent flippers (Section 1)** — direct end buyers; quick to reach by phone.
3. **Hard-money lenders (Section 2)** — one relationship can unlock dozens of qualified borrowers; ask them to forward your opt-in.
4. **REIAs (Section 3)** — attend the next BPM REIA / BREIA event; collect cards and opt-ins in volume.
5. **National brands / iBuyers** — backstop only; iBuyers likely pass on a full-gut.

**Relationship-building message (NO live deal — opt-in only):**
> "Hi [Name], I'm a local wholesaler focused on west Fort Lauderdale and the 33311/33309/33312/33313/33304 zips — mostly small SFR fix-and-flip and BRRRR-grade deals. I'm building a short, vetted buyers list so I can send the right deals to the right people. Can I learn your buy box and add you to my deal alerts? No spam — just deals that match what you actually buy. What's your sweet spot (price range, areas, condition, max rehab, financing/timeline)?"

**Qualification before list entry (buy-box capture):** target zips, property type, price range, condition tolerance / max rehab, financing (cash vs. hard money), close speed, monthly deal volume, contact preference, opt-in confirmed.

**POF gate (when a real deal goes live):** Require **current Proof of Funds or a lender pre-approval/term sheet before disclosing the property address** or full deal package. Until then share only generic criteria (e.g., "3/1 CBS, full gut, west Ft. Lauderdale, sub-$120K assignment"). Never publish an uncontracted address.

**Anti-spam:** Opt-in only; honor opt-outs; keep texts/emails compliant (clear identity, easy unsubscribe). Don't add cold-scraped contacts to automated blasts without consent.
