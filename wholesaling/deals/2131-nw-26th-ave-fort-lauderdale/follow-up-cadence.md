# Follow-Up Cadence — 2131 NW 26th Ave, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33311

**Prepared for:** the wholesaler (you) to execute personally
**Status:** Deal NOT under contract. Numbers are conservative planning estimates, not warranties.
**Companion assets (already drafted — reference, don't recreate):** offer cover note, listing-agent call script + objection handling, buyer outreach message pack.

---

## Standing Guardrails (apply to every message, every track)

- **Identify yourself every time.** You are a **real estate investor/buyer, NOT a licensed agent**, and you **intend to assign the contract** to another buyer. Never imply agency or brokerage representation.
- **Public offer is $100,000.** If you ever step up, move in **small increments only** ([+$2K–$3K]). **NEVER reveal or hint at the ~$115K walk-away ceiling** — not in writing, not on a call, not "between us."
- **Compliance:**
  - *Calls/texts (TCPA + Florida Telephone Solicitation Act / FTSA):* contact only people who fit a permissible basis (agent on an active public listing; buyers who **opted in**). Texts go during **8am–8pm local**, include your name, and offer **opt-out ("Reply STOP")**. When in doubt, call the agent rather than mass-text.
  - *Email (CAN-SPAM):* truthful subject lines, your identity + a physical mailing address in the footer, and a working **unsubscribe**.
- **Never share the property address with buyers** until POF is verified **AND** the property is under contract.
- **Stay in lane.** This cadence is communication/scheduling only. It does **not** change price, contract terms, or the buy/no-buy decision. **Any counter at/above the ceiling, or any term change, escalates to you (the human) — the cadence pauses until you decide.**
- All bracketed `[PLACEHOLDERS]` get filled in at send time.

---

## General Cadence Rules

- **Touch count before you stop:** Active negotiation (Track 1) — up to ~6 structured touches, then drop to long-tail monthly backup. Buyer list-building (Track 2) — 3 touches to a non-responder, then quarterly. Disposition (Track 3) — fast and finite, ~3–4 days.
- **Best times:** Calls/texts **Tue–Thu, 10am–12pm or 2pm–5pm local** convert best; avoid Mon AM, Fri PM, weekends, holidays. Hard window 8am–8pm.
- **Spacing:** Never two touches same day on the same channel. Alternate channels (call → text → email) so it reads as persistence, not pestering.
- **Never harass:** A clear "no," a STOP/unsubscribe, or "remove me" ends the cadence immediately and permanently. Honor it the same day.
- **Always leave a door open:** Even on a rejection, the last line invites them back ("I'm here if anything changes").

## TRACK 1 — LISTING AGENT / SELLER

**Goal:** Keep the $100K cash, as-is offer alive and top-of-mind; convert on a price drop, stale DOM, or a failed retail buyer. Primary contact is the **listing agent**, not the seller directly.

| Touch | Day | Channel | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 0 | Email (+ call) | Submit offer, confirm receipt |
| 2 | Day 2 | Call | Confirm it reached seller, answer questions |
| 3 | Day 5 | Text | Light nudge, reaffirm certainty of close |
| 4 | Day 9 | Email | Re-state value (cash, as-is, fast), no price move |
| 5 | Day 14 | Call | Status check; ask what gap looks like |
| 6 | Day 21 | Email | "Standing offer" — position as backup |
| 7+ | Monthly | Call or Email | Long-tail backup check-in |

### Day 0 — Email (submit) + courtesy call
> **Subject:** Cash offer — 2131 NW 26th Ave (as-is, quick close)
> Hi [AGENT NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME], a local real estate investor (not an agent — I buy and may assign). I've just submitted a written cash offer of **$100,000, as-is**, on 2131 NW 26th Ave. No financing or appraisal contingency, flexible close on your seller's timeline, and I cover [TITLE/CLOSING ITEMS]. Cover note + proof of funds attached. Can you confirm receipt and let me know the best way to reach your seller's decision? Thanks — [YOUR NAME], [PHONE].

*Call same day:* "Hi [AGENT NAME], [YOUR NAME] — just emailed a cash as-is offer on 26th Ave, wanted to make sure it landed and see if you have any quick questions."

### Day 2 — Call
> "Hi [AGENT NAME], [YOUR NAME] on the 26th Ave cash offer. Just checking it got in front of your seller — happy to walk through how clean and fast this closes. What's their read so far?"
*If voicemail:* short, name + property + "call me at [PHONE], no rush."

### Day 5 — Text (only if agent texts / opted to)
> [AGENT NAME], [YOUR NAME] here re: 2131 NW 26th Ave. Still ready to close cash, as-is on your seller's timeline. Anything you need from me? Reply STOP to opt out. — [YOUR NAME], [PHONE]

### Day 9 — Email (reaffirm value, hold price)
> **Subject:** Still ready — 2131 NW 26th Ave
> Hi [AGENT NAME], following up on my **$100,000 cash, as-is** offer. The value here is certainty: no lender, no appraisal, no repair requests, and I close when your seller wants. If there's anything blocking a yes, I'd like to hear it. — [YOUR NAME], [PHONE]

### Day 14 — Call (probe the gap, DO NOT reveal ceiling)
> "Hi [AGENT NAME], [YOUR NAME]. Where do things stand on 26th Ave? If we're apart on price, tell me roughly where their head is and I'll see what I can do — no promises."
- If they name a number: **do not counter on the call.** Say "Let me look at the numbers and circle back." Then escalate to the human. Any step-up is **small** ([+$2K–$3K] at a time) and **always stays well under the ceiling**.

### Day 21 — Email (convert to standing/backup offer)
> **Subject:** Standing cash offer on file — 2131 NW 26th Ave
> Hi [AGENT NAME], I understand $100K may not be where your seller is today. I'd like to leave my **cash, as-is offer on file as a backup**. If a retail buyer ties it up and falls out on inspection, appraisal, or financing, I'm a clean, fast safety net — no contingencies. Just keep my number handy: [PHONE]. — [YOUR NAME]

### Day 30+ — Long-tail monthly backup (call or email, alternate)
> "Hi [AGENT NAME], [YOUR NAME] checking in on 2131 NW 26th Ave — still here as a cash backup if anything wobbles on a retail contract. Any movement on your end?"

**Backup long game (when they list-price reject):**
- Watch DOM and price drops. **Trigger a touch** on: any price reduction, a listing going back-active after a contract falls through, or every ~30 days of staleness.
- On a price drop, you may re-present $100K (or a small step-up) framed as "the number that actually closes."

**When to walk away for good:**
- Seller signs with a retail buyer **and** that deal closes → archive.
- Agent says "remove me / stop contacting" → stop immediately, mark **Dead**.
- The only path to yes requires a price **at or above your ~$115K ceiling** → walk. Tell them: "I can't make the numbers work at that price, but my $100K cash backup stands if anything changes." **Do not chase past the ceiling.**
- ~90 days of no movement and no price flexibility → drop to quarterly or archive.

## TRACK 2 — BUYERS LIST (relationship / opt-in building)

**Goal:** Build and warm a verified cash-buyers list over time so you can move a deal fast later. **Deal is NOT under contract — share NO address, NO specific deal.** Everything here is opt-in and relationship-only.

| Touch | Timing | Channel | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 0 | Email or Call | Intro + invite to opt in to deal alerts |
| 2 | Day 4 | Call/VM or Text* | Follow up non-responders |
| 3 | Day 10 | Email | Value-add touch (build credibility) |
| 4 | Quarterly | Email | Stay-in-touch / criteria refresh |

\*Text only to numbers that have **opted in** or are existing business contacts.

### Touch 1 — Intro (email/call)
> Hi [BUYER NAME], I'm [YOUR NAME], a local investor sourcing off-market and value-add deals in **Broward / Fort Lauderdale**. I'm building a short list of serious cash buyers to send deals to first. If you'd like in, reply with your **buy box** (areas, price range, property type, and whether you pay cash). I only send real deals, never spam. — [YOUR NAME], [PHONE]
> *(If they reply, that reply is their opt-in — log the date.)*

### Touch 2 — Follow up non-responders (call/VM, or text if opted-in)
> *VM:* "Hi [BUYER NAME], [YOUR NAME] again — investor working Broward deals. Wanted to see if you want on my cash-buyer list. Quick reply with your criteria and I'll keep you posted. [PHONE]."
> *Text (opted-in only):* [BUYER NAME], [YOUR NAME] here — building my Broward cash-buyer list. Want on it? Reply with your buy box. Reply STOP to opt out.

### Touch 3 — Value-add (email)
> **Subject:** What's moving in Broward right now
> Hi [BUYER NAME], quick value note — I'm seeing [GENERAL MARKET OBSERVATION, e.g., flips in Rock Island / 33311 trading around $X/sqft]. No pitch, just keeping my buyers sharp. When I have a deal that fits your box, you'll be among the first to see it. Want me to fine-tune what I send you? — [YOUR NAME]

### Touch 4 — Quarterly stay-in-touch (email)
> **Subject:** Still buying? Let's keep your criteria current
> Hi [BUYER NAME], checking in — is your buy box still [AREAS / PRICE / TYPE]? Reply with any changes and I'll keep matching you to the right deals. — [YOUR NAME], [PHONE]
> *Footer (CAN-SPAM):* [YOUR NAME / BUSINESS], [MAILING ADDRESS]. Don't want these? Reply UNSUBSCRIBE.

**Notes:** A buyer who shares a buy box / says "yes, send me deals" = opted in (log it). No address, no "2131 NW 26th Ave," no numbers tied to a specific property until Track 3 activates. STOP/UNSUBSCRIBE honored same day.

## TRACK 3 — DISPOSITION

> ## ⛔ DO NOT USE UNTIL THE PROPERTY IS UNDER CONTRACT ⛔
> This track activates the moment the assignment-eligible contract is **signed**. Before that, you have nothing to sell and must not share the address, price, or deal specifics. Sharing the address requires **POF verified AND under contract**.

**Goal:** Move the deal fast inside the inspection/due-diligence window. Drive opted-in cash buyers to submit **POF + offer** quickly, hold a firm deadline, select the strongest, and keep a backup warm.

| Touch | Day | Channel | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Day 0 (contract signed) | Email + Text* | Blast the deal teaser, request POF |
| 2 | Day 1 | Call/VM | Push hottest buyers to submit |
| 3 | Day 2 | Text/Email | Offer-deadline reminder (firm) |
| 4 | Day 3 | Call | Select buyer; confirm with POF-verified |
| 5 | Day 3 | Call/Text | Warm the backup buyer |

\*Text opted-in buyers only; include STOP.

### Touch 1 — Day 0 blast (email + opted-in text)
> **Subject:** New Broward deal — cash, fast close (POF to unlock)
> Hi [BUYER NAME], [YOUR NAME] here. I've got a **Fort Lauderdale (33311 / Rock Island) 3/1 CBS, full-gut**, under contract and available for **assignment**. Conservative numbers: **ARV ~$330K, est. rehab ~$115K, my ask ~$116K** (estimates, not warranties). Send **proof of funds** and I'll release the full address + access details. Serious cash buyers only — this moves fast. — [YOUR NAME], [PHONE]
> *Text:* New Broward off-market flip, under contract, assignment. ~$116K. Reply with POF for address. — [YOUR NAME]. Reply STOP to opt out.
> **Do not include the street address in the blast.** Release it only to a buyer after POF is verified.

### Touch 2 — Day 1 call/VM (push hot buyers)
> "Hi [BUYER NAME], [YOUR NAME] — the 33311 gut-rehab assignment. If you're interested, get me your POF today so I can send the address and you can run it. Offers are coming in. [PHONE]."

### Touch 3 — Day 2 firm deadline (text/email)
> [BUYER NAME], [YOUR NAME] — offers on the Broward deal are due **[DEADLINE DATE/TIME]**. Need your number + POF by then to be considered. Reply STOP to opt out. — [YOUR NAME]
> *Hold the deadline.* Don't extend just because someone's "almost ready."

### Touch 4 — Day 3 select + confirm (call to POF-verified buyer)
> "Hi [BUYER NAME], good news — you've got it. I'll send the **assignment agreement** and we'll coordinate your earnest money to [TITLE/ESCROW CO] by [DATE]. Inspection access: [DETAILS]. Anything you need from me?"

### Touch 5 — Day 3 warm the backup (call/text)
> "Hi [BUYER NAME], [YOUR NAME] — I went with another buyer on this one, but you were close and I'd like to keep you as **backup** in case it doesn't close. Cool to keep your POF on file? More deals coming." — keeps the relationship and a fallback alive.

**Disposition rules:** Verify every POF before releasing the address (a name on a bank letter that matches the buyer, recent date, sufficient funds). Collect earnest money from the assignee per your contract. If the inspection window is short, compress the cadence (Day 0 / Day 1 / Day 2-deadline). If no qualified buyer surfaces before your contingency expires → escalate to the human to decide on extend, renegotiate, or terminate.

## Cadence-Tracking Table Schema

Run the whole operation off one sheet. One row per contact per track.

| Column | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| **Contact Name** | Person's name |
| **Role/Company** | Listing agent / Seller / Buyer + brokerage or firm |
| **Track** | 1 (Agent/Seller) · 2 (Buyer list) · 3 (Disposition) |
| **Channel (preferred)** | Call / Text / Email |
| **Phone / Email** | Best contact(s) |
| **Opt-in status** | (Track 2/3) Opted-in Y/N + date; or N/A |
| **POF status** | (Track 3) Not requested / Requested / Verified |
| **Last Touch Date** | Date of most recent contact |
| **Last Touch (channel + outcome)** | e.g., "Call — VM left" / "Email — replied" |
| **Next Touch Date** | When the next touch is due |
| **Next Touch (channel + goal)** | Planned channel + objective |
| **Status** | Active · Warm · Backup · Negotiating · Won · Dead/Opted-out |
| **Notes** | Counters heard, buy box, price drops, anything to escalate |

**Operating tip:** Sort by **Next Touch Date** each morning. Anything marked **Dead/Opted-out** is frozen — no further contact, ever.
