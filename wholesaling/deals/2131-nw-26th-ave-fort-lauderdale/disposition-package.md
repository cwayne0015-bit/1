## 5. Buyer Qualification & Selection

Protect the deal: you want the buyer who actually closes, not the highest number from someone who ghosts on day 10 of your contract window.

**Qualify every responder on three things:**
1. **Proof of Funds (POF) — mandatory before address/access.**
   - Acceptable: dated bank/brokerage statement (last 30 days), hard-money lender pre-approval/POF letter, or transactional funding commitment.
   - Red flags: "I have a partner with the money," generic/undated screenshots, POF below the all-in number, requests to wholesale it further without proof.
2. **Track record / certainty signals.**
   - How many deals in the last 12 months? Any in 33311/Broward? Closed all-cash before? Own crews or a GC lined up for a full gut?
   - A buyer who already bought a near-twin (e.g., the 2798 NW 20th St type of buyer) is gold.
3. **Earnest money commitment.**
   - Confirm they'll wire **$[AMOUNT] non-refundable to [ESCROW]** at assignment. Willingness to post hard EMD is the single best predictor of a real close.

**How to weigh price vs. certainty of close:**
- Your contract window is finite. A backup that falls through can cost you the whole deal. **Certainty > a few thousand dollars of extra price.**
- Rank offers on a simple matrix: (a) POF verified, (b) hard non-refundable EMD, (c) close date inside your window with cushion, (d) track record, (e) price.
- Take the strongest combination — typically the proven local cash flipper at a slightly lower number over an unproven buyer waving a bigger one.
- **Always line up a backup buyer (#2)** and keep them warm in case #1 defaults.
- **Do NOT take the property off-market / commit until POF is verified and EMD terms are agreed.** Tag them `POF-Verified` first.

**Guardrail:** if no buyer's math works at your $116K ask, do not paper over it or inflate ARV to force a sale — drop the assignment fee toward your floor, or renegotiate your acquisition. Never send numbers you can't defend; the list remembers.
